# Candidate cross-fold support audit implementation closeout

BF-529 through BF-532 implement Butler's first governed **candidate cross-fold support and directional-consistency audit** over the BF-526 candidate-threshold study.

This layer describes evidence breadth and raw held-out displacement direction. It does **not** select, score, rank, optimize, fit, refine, or publish a candidate threshold.

## Implemented surfaces

- BF-529 normative methodology: [`league-lineup-capture-ranking-sensitivity-candidate-cross-fold-support-methodology.md`](league-lineup-capture-ranking-sensitivity-candidate-cross-fold-support-methodology.md)
- BF-530 analyzer/report: `LeagueLineupCaptureRankingSensitivityCandidateCrossFoldSupportAuditAnalyzer`
- BF-531 CLI:

```text
butler league lineup-capture-ranking-sensitivity-candidate-cross-fold-support-audit <start-season> <end-season>
```

- BF-532 global help and durable closeout

## Implemented policy

```text
league-lineup-capture-ranking-sensitivity-candidate-cross-fold-support-v1-cluster-aware-breadth-and-direction-audit-no-selection-no-score-no-confidence
```

The BF-530 implementation consumes the governed BF-526 `CandidateThresholdStudyReport` directly. BF-526 remains the sole candidate/fold source of truth.

If BF-526 is unavailable, the support audit fails closed as `UNAVAILABLE_CANDIDATE_STUDY` and publishes no partial candidate support rows.

## Primary evidence unit remains league-season

The audit cluster remains:

```text
league-id + season
```

Team-cutoff rows remain correlated within a league-season and are never treated as independent sample N.

For each candidate, BF-530 preserves explicit counts for:

- total BF-526 folds;
- generated folds;
- `NOT_GENERATED_IN_DEVELOPMENT_FOLD` folds;
- `EVALUABLE` folds; and
- `UNEVALUABLE_NO_HELD_OUT_SPLIT` folds.

These are structural support counts, not statistical adequacy claims.

## Support breadth states

BF-530 implements exactly four deterministic breadth labels:

```text
NO_EVALUABLE_FOLDS
SINGLE_EVALUABLE_FOLD
MULTI_FOLD_NARROW_SUPPORT
MULTI_FOLD_DIVERSE_SUPPORT
```

`NO_EVALUABLE_FOLDS` means the candidate has no evaluable held-out league-season fold.

`SINGLE_EVALUABLE_FOLD` means exactly one held-out league-season fold is evaluable.

`MULTI_FOLD_NARROW_SUPPORT` means at least two folds are evaluable but the governed diversity requirements are not all met.

`MULTI_FOLD_DIVERSE_SUPPORT` requires at least two evaluable held-out league-season identities and at least two repository team-count strata. Frequency candidates additionally require at least two perturbation-denominator values represented on both held-out rule sides across their evaluable evidence.

These labels describe **evidence breadth only**. They are not confidence levels, probability estimates, sample-size adequacy findings, quality grades, or selection permissions.

## Breadth diagnostics remain explicit

For each evaluable candidate, BF-530 preserves distinct:

- held-out league IDs;
- held-out seasons;
- held-out league-season identities;
- repository team-count strata; and
- for frequency candidates, perturbation denominators represented on both rule sides.

The implementation never collapses those contexts into a scalar support score.

## Raw directional displacement evidence

For every evaluable candidate/fold pair, BF-530 compares the already-published BF-526 rule sides using raw total absolute temporal rank displacement.

The only direction states are:

```text
MEETS_SIDE_LOWER_TOTAL_ABSOLUTE_DISPLACEMENT
MEETS_SIDE_HIGHER_TOTAL_ABSOLUTE_DISPLACEMENT
EQUAL_TOTAL_ABSOLUTE_DISPLACEMENT
```

Each total is reconstructed from the full BF-526 absolute displacement distribution:

```text
sum(displacement_value * row_count)
```

The report preserves both source side summaries beside that raw comparison, including each side's row count and full absolute/signed displacement distributions.

Raw totals are sensitive to unequal side row counts. BF-530 therefore does not normalize them into displacement-per-row, mean/median loss, accuracy, win rate, or any other scalar objective.

## Direction-count summary remains descriptive

Across evaluable folds, BF-530 may count how many folds fall into each direction state and expose the ordered fold-direction rows.

Those counts are never converted into:

- a win percentage;
- probability or confidence;
- significance;
- a support score;
- a candidate objective; or
- a candidate ranking.

## Candidate identity and ordering remain governed by BF-526

BF-530/BF-531 preserve BF-526 candidate identities and ordering exactly.

Frequency candidates remain exact canonical rational identities generated by development evidence. Magnitude candidates remain observed integer maximum-movement cutoffs.

The support audit does not:

- interpolate or merge candidate values;
- create midpoint thresholds;
- reverse candidate directions;
- combine magnitude and frequency;
- reorder candidates by support state;
- reorder candidates by direction counts;
- reorder candidates by raw displacement totals; or
- break candidate ties using apparent held-out performance.

## CLI evidence surface

BF-531 exposes the support audit through the centralized Butler router.

For an available report, the CLI prints:

- report and BF-526 source states;
- BF-526-ordered frequency and magnitude candidates;
- cluster-level fold counts;
- breadth diagnostics;
- support states;
- fold-direction counts;
- ordered per-fold direction states; and
- each rule side's raw BF-526 diagnostics.

For an unavailable BF-526 source, the CLI publishes no candidate support evidence and repeats the stop boundary.

## What remains unauthorized

BF-529 through BF-532 do not authorize:

- defining a scalar candidate objective;
- normalizing displacement into a candidate score;
- ranking candidates by apparent held-out performance;
- automatically selecting a candidate;
- selecting every `MULTI_FOLD_DIVERSE_SUPPORT` candidate;
- tie-breaking candidates by support breadth or direction counts;
- production threshold publication;
- threshold interpolation, fitting, or refinement;
- calibrated sensitivity categories;
- probability of future rank retention or movement;
- confidence in a baseline rank or candidate threshold;
- confidence intervals, statistical significance, bootstrap confidence, or Bayesian posterior probability;
- confidence-adjusted or sensitivity-adjusted BF-500 ranks;
- manager consistency, reliability, skill, quality, or fault grades;
- sensitivity leaderboards;
- recommendations; or
- cross-league manager comparisons.

Even a candidate with `MULTI_FOLD_DIVERSE_SUPPORT` and lower raw total absolute displacement on every evaluable fold remains **not selected** and **not production-authorized**.

## Source integrity and fail-closed behavior

BF-530 report records recompute their governed fields from the BF-526 source and reject fabricated availability or inconsistent audit fields.

The implementation does not reconstruct missing BF-526 folds, weaken BF-521/BF-525 prerequisites, synthesize candidate identities, or use held-out rows to expand development candidate vocabularies.

## UX/product guardrail remains separate

The analytical support audit is an evidence surface, not a mandate to show every diagnostic by default in future product interfaces. Product UX constraints remain separate from this methodology and do not change the analytical stop boundary.

## Governed stop after BF-532

BF-529 explicitly ends the authorized sequence at BF-532.

After this closeout, a **new governed methodology decision is required** before Butler may define any scalar objective, normalized candidate score, automatic selection rule, candidate performance ordering, production threshold, calibrated category, confidence/probability model, adjusted rank, manager score, sensitivity leaderboard, or recommendation.

No BF-533 candidate-selection implementation should proceed merely because the BF-530/BF-531 evidence can now be inspected.

The next fork is methodological: decide whether a defensible objective exists at all, given the small clustered corpus, unequal candidate/fold availability, side-row imbalance, and the fact that future-only ordinal rank movement is descriptive persistence evidence rather than ground truth.
